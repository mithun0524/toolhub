    Frequently asked questions

    Silent reading is estimated at 225 words per minute, the average for adults reading non-technical prose. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a comfortable presentation pace — useful when you are writing a talk or a video script.
    A sentence is a run of text closed by a full stop, question mark, exclamation mark or ellipsis — and trailing text with no punctuation still counts, so a single unpunctuated line reads as one sentence. Paragraphs are blocks separated by a blank line, matching how writers actually structure documents.
