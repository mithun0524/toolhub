Frequently asked questions

    

      
      
The standard reducing-balance EMI formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the principal, r the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of months. A 0% loan is handled separately as simply P ÷ n, because the formula divides by zero at that rate.
